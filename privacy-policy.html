Privacy Policy for DerDieDas

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Lef ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile game DerDieDas (the "Game").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use DerDieDas.

1. Information We Collect

DerDieDas does not require users to create an account or log in.

We do not directly collect, store, or request personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, home address, photographs, or other information that directly identifies you.

The Game does not provide in-app purchases (IAP).

The Game also does not use Firebase Analytics or Google Analytics.

2. Advertising

DerDieDas uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements and interstitial advertisements.

Google AdMob is a third-party advertising service. In order to deliver, measure, maintain, and improve advertisements, Google and its advertising partners may process certain information associated with your device and your use of the Game.

Depending on the device, settings, location, applicable laws, and advertising configuration, this may include information such as:

  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Device type and operating system information
  • Advertising and ad interaction information
  • Information used to detect fraud and maintain advertising security

We do not directly receive or use this information to identify you personally. The processing of information by Google is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

For more information about how Google uses data when you use applications or websites that use its services, please visit:

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

Google AdMob may use information for personalized or non-personalized advertising depending on applicable laws, user choices, device settings, and the configuration of the advertising service.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate consent mechanisms may be used for advertising and related data processing.

Users may also have options provided by their device or Google to manage advertising personalization and related privacy settings.

4. Children's Privacy

DerDieDas is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Game.

5. Data Storage

DerDieDas does not operate a user account system and does not store user personal information on our own servers.

The Game does not use a backend service to collect or store personal user information.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Since we do not directly collect or maintain personal information through user accounts or backend databases, there is no personal account data maintained by Lef that requires deletion.

Information processed by third-party advertising providers such as Google AdMob is subject to the respective provider's privacy policies, retention practices, and applicable legal requirements.
